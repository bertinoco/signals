As a Staff Content Developer, you will help customers get more value from 1Password by creating content that supports product usage, adoption, retention, and expansion.
You will join the Customer Content team and report to the Manager, Customer Content. This is a hands-on role that works across Product Marketing, Product, Customer Success, Customer Support, Lifecycle Marketing, Enablement, and other teams. You will bring strong content judgment, an understanding of customer needs, and a practical approach to improving how content is created, maintained, and optimized.
How we're using AI today
Across GTM, we're transforming the way we work with AI. We expect every seller, CSM, and team member to become a power user, using it daily to work smarter and move faster. AI is already embedded in how we operate – from real-time coaching that sharpens how we sell and interact with customers, to surfacing insights that help us anticipate needs – creating more time for meaningful customer engagement. If you're excited to reshape how you think, make decisions, and drive growth with AI, you'll feel right at home here.
This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US.
What we're looking for:
5+ years of experience in B2B SaaS content marketing, customer marketing, or a related field.
Ability to translate complex products, workflows, and technical concepts into clear, actionable content for technical audiences across programs, formats, and stages of the customer journey.
Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and changing requirements.
A self-directed, adaptable approach to work, with the judgment to make progress independently and the openness to incorporate feedback.
Experience with content management systems, analytics tools, collaboration platforms, or AI-assisted content workflows is helpful, but not required.
Strong editorial judgment and attention to accuracy, structure, accessibility, voice, and detail.
Experience managing content through its full lifecycle, including development reviews, repurposing, optimization, and retirement.
Experience using qualitative and quantitative inputs to evaluate content and guide improvements.
A history of creating practical processes, templates, or systems that improve the quality and efficiency of content work.
What you can expect:
Develop customer-facing content from brief to final delivery, including research, outlining, drafting, editing, review coordination, and publishing.
Create content for lifecycle programs, enablement, multimedia, and other customer-facing channels.
Make complex product capabilities, workflows, and technical concepts clear, relevant, and actionable.
Adapt messaging, structure, tone, and format for different audiences, use cases, and stages of the customer journey.
Work with subject matter experts and cross-functional partners to gather information, resolve questions, and incorporate feedback.
Keep content accurate and useful as products, policies, customer needs, and program priorities change.
Create reusable templates, content modules, editorial guidance, and workflows that make production and maintenance more efficient.
Use customer feedback, support themes, search behavior, product usage signals, and performance data to recommend and make improvements.
Track and interpret content performance, then apply learnings to future work.
Manage multiple projects, timelines, reviews, and stakeholders while maintaining a high standard of quality.
Use AI and other tools responsibly to support research, drafting, production, maintenance, and optimization.
Share observations and recommendations that help the Customer Content team improve its plans and priorities.
