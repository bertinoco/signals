AI-Driven Digital Communicator
We're looking for a tech-savvy language and digital communication specialist, eager to navigate the evolving media landscape through hands-on consulting assignments in a fast-moving environment.

Who we believe you are

You see AI as more than a tool – it's your creative partner. You love breaking down complex communication challenges and rebuilding them with smart, automated workflows, but you never lose sight of what matters most: the message and the strategic context. You navigate with confidence in today's rapidly changing communication landscape and understand how modern digital channels function together.

You're genuinely curious about technology, but at your core, you are rooted in the craft of communication. Whether your background is in copywriting, digital marketing, or language studies, you bring a strong foundation in foundational communication principles. You know that even the most advanced tech requires human precision, adaptability, and clear writing to truly resonate. Whether you're just starting out or have a few years under your belt, you combine broad digital communication skills with an appetite for exploring AI, LLMs, and automated workflows from the inside.


What we're looking for

1–5 years of professional experience in digital communication, copywriting, content production, or tech.
Relevant education in communication, language/linguistics, media, digital marketing, or copywriting.
Strong communication and linguistic skills. You have a solid grasp of storytelling, copy editing, and tone of voice adaptation. You understand digital strategy and how content moves across different platforms.
Advanced AI prompt engineering. You can steer LLMs and generative tools with precision. For you, natural language is the ultimate interface and code that drives AI.
Technical depth. You have experience with, or a genuine drive to master, complex automation tools, LLM frameworks, and low-code environments such as Claude, Make, OpenAI APIs, or LangChain.
Linguistic versatility. You speak and write Swedish fluently, and professional-level English. You're a language person who happens to love tech.
A consultant mindset. You're proactive, solution-oriented, and energized by varied, fast-paced assignments.

What you can expect in your assignments

Drive broad digital communication initiatives. Create, edit, and optimize content for various digital channels, relying on both traditional communication methods and modern toolsets.
Engineer high-quality copy and content. Combine advanced prompting with your core copywriting and editorial skills to produce written assets that meet the highest professional standards.
Develop AI workflows. Design and build automated content pipelines that enhance creative quality and copy precision, without losing the human element.
Bridge tech and creative. Act as the ultimate translator between technical AI capabilities and language/creative goals, making sure the technology always serves the story.
Experiment hands-on. Continuously test new language models and multimodal AI tools to keep Curious Mind at the forefront, always evaluating them through a linguist's and communicator's lens.
Collaborate and educate. Work closely with colleagues and clients, helping them refine their prompt frameworks and leverage AI effectively without losing their unique voice.

What Curious Mind can offer you

We're content consultants born in the digital era driven by strategy, thoughtful production, and a passion for innovation. With us, you'll enjoy a varied professional life at a company that values expertise, personality, and genuine connection. If you're curious and driven by growth, you'll feel right at home here.

Some of what we're offering you:

Access to a support system of experts and personal coaches
A culture that actively promotes caring and supportive leadership
Competitive compensation package
Access to Eidra's network of specialists, from brand strategy to tech development
If you have any questions, please contact Ville Krantz, ville.krantz@curiousmind.se. And if you would like to know more about Curious Mind, please feel free to visit our website. We interview on an ongoing basis.
