OverviewApplication
Virta Health is on a mission to reverse metabolic disease in one billion people. Current treatment approaches aren't working—over half of US adults have either type 2 diabetes or prediabetes, and obesity rates are at an all-time high. Virta is changing this by helping people reverse their metabolic condition through innovations in technology, personalized nutrition, and virtual care delivery reinvented from the ground up. We have raised over $350 million from top-tier investors, and partner with the largest health plans, employers, and government organizations to help their employees and members restore their health and take back their lives. Join us on our mission to reverse metabolic disease in one billion people.
As a Senior Content Designer, you'll create engaging content and digital experiences that help Virta reach new audiences and support members throughout their health journey. You'll own content design for assigned product initiatives, from onboarding and education to personalized guidance and AI-powered conversations that inspire action and lasting behavior change. Partnering closely with Product, Design, Engineering, and cross-functional teams, you'll bring exceptional writing, UX design, behavioral science, and generative AI expertise to make complex concepts clear and actionable. You'll also contribute to scalable content systems that help deliver the right information to the right members at the right time. This is an opportunity to create something new, help shape the future of AI-powered member experiences, and make a meaningful impact on how people understand and improve their health.

Responsibilities
Product content strategy & creation

* Own content strategy and execution for assigned product experiences, helping Virta reach new audiences and engage members across different needs, goals, and stages of their health journey.
* Create clear, compelling content across onboarding, product features, in-app messaging, articles, educational resources, and interactive experiences.
* Concept and develop innovative content formats that make complex health concepts approachable, engaging, and actionable.
* Partner with Product, Design, and Engineering to shape experiences from early discovery through launch and optimization.
* Apply UX writing, storytelling, behavioral science, and audience insights to build confidence, inspire action, and improve member engagement.

Content architecture & personalization

* Contribute to scalable content structures, taxonomies, metadata, and labeling systems that make content easy to discover, manage, and personalize.
* Partner with Product and Engineering to structure and implement content in Sanity, Virta's CMS, helping deliver relevant content to members at the right time.
* Support content models and workflows that enable personalized experiences while maintaining quality and consistency.

Conversational AI & content quality

* Design the voice, tone, and conversational experience for assigned AI-powered product features, ensuring interactions feel natural, engaging, helpful, and distinctly Virta.
* Develop and refine LLM prompts, instructions, and conversational frameworks that guide AI-generated responses.
* Partner with Product, Engineering, and AI teams to design and improve conversational experiences that help members find answers and take meaningful next steps.
* Develop and apply evaluation criteria to improve AI-generated content for clarity, usefulness, empathy, and brand alignment.
* Collaborate with Clinical and Care Delivery teams, who provide authoritative health guidance and own clinical accuracy.

90 Day Plan
Within your first 90 days at Virta, we expect you will do the following:

* Develop a strong understanding of Virta's members, care model, brand voice, product experience, business priorities, and key measures of success.
* Build strong working relationships across Product, Design, Engineering, Clinical, Lifecycle, and other key partners.
* Assess existing product content, member research, AI-powered experiences, and content infrastructure, including Sanity, to identify opportunities to improve the member experience and reach new audiences.
* Contribute to the content strategy and execution of a priority product initiative, creating clear, engaging content and experiences that help members learn, take action, and achieve their goals.
* Evaluate existing conversational AI experiences, including prompting, voice, and response quality, and identify opportunities to improve clarity, usefulness, and consistency.
* Develop an initial point of view on content architecture, taxonomy, and personalization opportunities, partnering with Engineering to identify improvements that help deliver more relevant content throughout the member journey.

Must-Haves

* 5+ years of experience in content design, UX writing, product content strategy, conversational design, or a related field.
* Exceptional writing, editing, and storytelling skills, with experience creating original content across articles, educational resources, and digital product experiences.
* Experience owning content design for digital product features or initiatives, with a strong understanding of user-centered design, information architecture, and content management systems.
* Hands-on experience designing conversational AI or LLM-powered experiences, including prompt development, model evaluation, and improving AI-generated content quality.
* Strong cross-functional collaboration skills, with experience partnering with Product, Design, and Engineering to build scalable content experiences and using research and analytics to improve engagement.
* Successfully designed and implemented repeatable AI-enabled workflows that address team bottlenecks and improve efficiency. For more information about AI usage in our hiring process, see Virta's AI Interview Resources page.

Values-driven culture
Virta's company values drive our culture, so you'll do well if:

* You put people first and take care of yourself, your peers, and our patients equally
* You have a strong sense of ownership and take initiative while empowering others to do the same
* You prioritize positive impact over busy work
* You have no ego and understand that everyone has something to bring to the table regardless of experience
* You appreciate transparency and promote trust and empowerment through open access of information
* You are evidence-based and prioritize data and science over seniority or dogma
* You take risks and rapidly iterate
