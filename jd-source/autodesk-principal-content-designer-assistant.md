Position Overview 
At Autodesk, we help our customers design and make anything. The Experience Foundations team creates the systems, standards, and experiences that enable that mission, particularly as AI transforms how customers work across increasingly connected products and workflows. 
Within Experience Foundations for AEC, we are seeking a Principal Content Designer focused on education and knowledge for Autodesk Assistant and related agentic experiences. This role will define how help, learning, and trusted product knowledge evolve for an AI-first world, ensuring customers can access the right guidance across products, workflows, and moments of need. 
This role requires deep expertise in content design, help and learning experiences, and content systems. The ideal candidate understands how agentic experiences change the way customers encounter knowledge, from contextual guidance and AI-generated responses to troubleshooting and deeper learning. Working across Product, Design, Engineering, Conversation Design, Research, and product content teams, they will create scalable content models and experiences that connect these modes into a cohesive ecosystem. 

Responsibilities 

* Define and evolve the help content strategy for education and knowledge across Autodesk Assistant and related agentic experiences

* Design content experiences that help customers learn, orient, troubleshoot, recover, and build expertise in the context of their work

* Define how education and guidance surface across onboarding, conversation, contextual guidance, recovery, and deeper learning

* Establish content models and patterns that enable authoritative knowledge to be structured and reused across products and AI experiences

* Partner with Product and Engineering to shape how trusted knowledge supports retrieval, grounding, and customer-facing guidance

* Create scalable frameworks that enable product and domain teams to contribute knowledge while maintaining quality and consistency

* Design patterns for AI-mediated learning, including contextual education, progressive guidance, explanations, and suggested next steps

* Identify fragmentation, duplication, and gaps across help and knowledge experiences and develop approaches that improve findability, reuse, and understanding

* Define and apply quality standards and evaluation approaches for help, learning, and AI-delivered knowledge

* Partner across disciplines to ensure education and knowledge work coherently across products and customer journeys


Minimum Qualifications 

* 7+ years of experience in content design, UX content, help content, learning experience design, technical content, information architecture, knowledge management, or a related discipline

* Demonstrated content design expertise, with a portfolio showing complex help, education, knowledge, or product experiences from strategy through implementation

* Experience creating scalable content approaches for product guidance, education, or knowledge experiences

* Strong understanding of how people learn within digital products, including onboarding, contextual guidance, troubleshooting, and progressive learning

* Experience designing structured, reusable content across products, surfaces, or contexts

* Strong information architecture skills and experience organizing complex knowledge

* Experience working within distributed content ecosystems with multiple contributors or subject-matter experts

* Demonstrated ability to independently frame ambiguous content problems and translate them into clear strategies, models, patterns, and experiences

* Familiarity with generative AI, retrieval, grounding, or knowledge systems

* Experience defining or applying quality standards and evaluation approaches to content

* Experience using AI tools to explore, prototype, evaluate, or improve content experiences

* Excellent communication and influence skills across disciplines and stakeholders


Preferred Qualifications 

* Experience designing content for conversational, generative AI, or agentic experiences

* Experience with structured content, taxonomy, metadata, content models, or reusable knowledge systems

* Familiarity with knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, MCP, or related knowledge architectures

* Experience designing contextual learning integrated directly into product experiences

* Experience with shared content systems, standards, or pattern libraries

* Experience designing content for localization and accessibility

* Experience with complex professional software or the AEC industry
